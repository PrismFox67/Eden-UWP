# AGENTS

Eden-UWP is a fork of Jadyx (Eden on UWP for Xbox). Read docs/PLAN.md and docs/NOTES.md first.

Read and follow the AI policy found in `docs/policies/AI.md`, and the coding policies found in `docs/policies/Coding.md`.
Jadyx's own agent guide (in Spanish) is kept at `docs/xbox/jadyx_AGENTS.md` for its build and test knowledge; its
rules about which repository to push to do not apply here.

Do not, under any circumstances, ever submit PRs or push commits on behalf of the user.

Never add Switch keys, firmware, games or anything derived from them. Keep every copyright notice and credit Jadyx,
juanresendiz813 and Eden for code taken from them.

Keep comments to a reasonable level. Do not overexplain or add/remove comments from unrelated parts of the codebase.
